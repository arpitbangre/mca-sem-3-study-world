## Question Paper Content

> **Note:** (1) All questions are compulsory and carry equal marks. (2) Draw neat and labelled diagrams wherever necessary.

### Question 1 (Unit I) [16 Marks]
#### EITHER
- **a)** What is Data Mining? Explain the Data Mining Tasks. `[8 Marks]`
- **b)** Explain-
  i) Variable Transformation
  ii) Discretization and Binarization `[8 Marks]`

#### OR
- **c)** Explain the issues related to Applications. `[8 Marks]`
- **d)** Explain-
  i) Attributes and Measurement
  ii) Types of data sets. `[8 Marks]`

### Question 2 (Unit II) [16 Marks]
#### EITHER
- **a)** Explain the concept of Iris Data Set. `[8 Marks]`
- **b)** Write a note on Multidimensional data Analysis. `[8 Marks]`

#### OR
- **c)** Explain –
  i) Percentiles
  ii) Mean and Median `[8 Marks]`
- **d)** Explain the concept of Decision Tree Induction. `[8 Marks]`

### Question 3 (Unit III) [16 Marks]
#### EITHER
- **a)** Explain the concept of Bayesian classifiers in detail. `[8 Marks]`
- **b)** Explain –
  i) Support Vector Machine
  ii) Naïve Bayes classifier. `[8 Marks]`

#### OR
- **c)** Write a note on-
  i) Apriori Principle
  ii) Rule Generation `[8 Marks]`
- **d)** What is FP – Tree Representation? Explain in detail. `[8 Marks]`

### Question 4 (Unit IV) [16 Marks]
#### EITHER
- **a)** Explain the concept of K-means and it’s variant. `[8 Marks]`
- **b)** Explain –
  i) Causes of Anomaly Detection.
  ii) Approaches to Anomaly Detection. `[8 Marks]`

#### OR
- **c)** What is cluster Analysis? Explain different types of clustering. `[8 Marks]`
- **d)** Explain Proximity – Based outlier Detection concept. `[8 Marks]`
