## Question Paper Content

> **Note:** (1) All questions are compulsory and carry equal marks. (2) Draw neat and labelled diagrams wherever necessary.

### Question 1 (Unit I) [16 Marks]
#### EITHER
- **a)** Explain A* algorithm with suitable example. `[8 Marks]`
- **b)** What is AI? Explain water jug problem. `[8 Marks]`

#### OR
- **c)** What is constraint satisfaction? Explain its algorithm. `[8 Marks]`
- **d)** Describe Hill climbing algorithm. What are its limitations. `[8 Marks]`

### Question 2 (Unit II) [16 Marks]
#### EITHER
- **a)** Explain issues of knowledge representation. `[8 Marks]`
- **b)** Write note on:
  i) Natural Deduction.
  ii) Logic programming. `[8 Marks]`

#### OR
- **c)** Write and explain forward versus backward reasoning. `[8 Marks]`
- **d)** What is predicate logic? Explain resolution in predicate logic. `[8 Marks]`

### Question 3 (Unit III) [16 Marks]
#### EITHER
- **a)** What are the components of planning system? Explain. `[8 Marks]`
- **b)** Explain Min-Max algorithm with an example. `[8 Marks]`

#### OR
- **c)** Discuss the additional refinements to the minimax procedure. `[8 Marks]`
- **d)** Explain the followings.
  i) Alpha beta cut off
  ii) Goal task planning `[8 Marks]`

### Question 4 (Unit IV) [16 Marks]
#### EITHER
- **a)** What is parallel AI? Explain Psychological modeling. `[8 Marks]`
- **b)** Explain Semantic analysis with example. `[8 Marks]`

#### OR
- **c)** Write notes on:
  i) Unification grammar
  ii) Syntactic processing. `[8 Marks]`
- **d)** Explain distributed reasoning systems in details. `[8 Marks]`

### Question 5 (Compulsory / Short Notes) [16 Marks - 4 Marks Each]
- **a)** Explain problem as a state space search. `[4 Marks]`
- **b)** Explain the concept of resolution. `[4 Marks]`
- **c)** Write note on Hierarchical Planning. `[4 Marks]`
- **d)** Write note on natural language processing. `[4 Marks]`
