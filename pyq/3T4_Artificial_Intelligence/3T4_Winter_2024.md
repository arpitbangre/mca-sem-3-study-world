## Question Paper Content

> **Note:** (1) All questions are compulsory and carry equal marks. (2) Draw neat and labelled diagrams wherever necessary.

### Question 1 (Unit I) [16 Marks]
#### EITHER
- **a)** Discuss the AI problem. List the different AI techniques used to solve it. `[8 Marks]`
- **b)** Describe Hill climbing algorithm. What are its limitation? `[8 Marks]`

#### OR
- **c)** Explain means-ends-analysis with example. `[8 Marks]`
- **d)** Explain briefly various problem characteristics. `[8 Marks]`

### Question 2 (Unit II) [16 Marks]
#### EITHER
- **a)** Differentiate between forward and backward reasoning. `[8 Marks]`
- **b)** Explain computable functions and predicates in detail. `[8 Marks]`

#### OR
- **c)** Discuss various methods of knowledge representation in detail. `[8 Marks]`
- **d)** Explain the following:
  i) Natural Deduction
  ii) Logic Programing `[8 Marks]`

### Question 3 (Unit III) [16 Marks]
#### EITHER
- **a)** Discuss the components of planning system. Why it is needed? `[8 Marks]`
- **b)** What is goal stack planning? Give its features. `[8 Marks]`

#### OR
- **c)** Elaborate MINIMAX search procedure in detail. `[8 Marks]`
- **d)** Explain alpha-beta cutoff's in game playing. `[8 Marks]`

### Question 4 (Unit IV) [16 Marks]
#### EITHER
- **a)** Explain unification grammars and semantic analysis. `[8 Marks]`
- **b)** Differentiate between parallel and distributed AI. `[8 Marks]`

#### OR
- **c)** Explain psychological modeling in detail. `[8 Marks]`
- **d)** What is Natural Language Processing? Give its features. `[8 Marks]`

### Question 5 (Compulsory / Short Notes) [16 Marks - 4 Marks Each]
- **a)** Explain Water Jug problem. `[4 Marks]`
- **b)** What is first order Logic? Explain. `[4 Marks]`
- **c)** Explain hierarchical planning with example. `[4 Marks]`
- **d)** Write a note on constraint satisfaction. `[4 Marks]`
