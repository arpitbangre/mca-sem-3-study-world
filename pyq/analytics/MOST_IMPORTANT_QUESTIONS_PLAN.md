## 📐 The RTMNU Mathematical Exam Strategy

Every theory paper follows a strict 80-mark structure:
- **Questions 1 to 4:** Strictly map to **Units I, II, III, and IV** respectively.
  - Each question offers an **Internal Choice**: **EITHER** `(a)` [8M] + `(b)` [8M] **OR** `(c)` [8M] + `(d)` [8M].
- **Question 5:** **Compulsory Short Notes** `(a, b, c, d)` at **4 Marks each = 16 Marks** (exactly one sub-question from each of the 4 units).
- **Total Questions to Answer:** 5 × 16 Marks = **80 Marks**.

### 💡 The Minimum Preparation Formula:
Because of the `EITHER / OR` structure in Questions 1–4:
- You only need to answer **EITHER (a)+(b) OR (c)+(d)** in each unit.
- Preparing **4 to 5 core archetypes per unit** covers **both** options and simultaneously answers the 4-mark compulsory question in Question 5!
- **Total Master Questions per Subject:** **16 to 18 High-Yield Questions**.
- **Total for entire MCA Sem 3 (5 Subjects):** **~85 Core Questions** guarantees scoring **70+ out of 80**.

## ✍️ RTMNU 16-Mark Answer Sheet Presentation Standard

Evaluators at RTMNU award top marks (14–16 out of 16) when answers follow this structure:
1. **Definition & Core Concept** (10–15% of space): Clear 2–3 line technical definition.
2. **Neat Architectural / Flow Diagram** (30% of space): Block diagram with labeled components.
3. **Working Principle / Algorithm Steps** (30% of space): Stepwise numbered procedure or mathematical formulation.
4. **Comparative Analysis / Example Trace** (20% of space): Markdown/matrix table comparing alternatives or a concrete walkthrough (e.g., trace of Water Jug, trace of Decision Tree, or derivation of EBPA weights).
5. **Applications & Significance** (5% of space): 2 bullet points on real-world use.
